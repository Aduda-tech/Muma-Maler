## 3. Full description

```
Muma Maler is the complete Holy Bible in Dholuo (Luo) — the full Muma Machon
(Old Testament) and Muma Manyien (New Testament), all 66 books and 31,102
verses, in one beautiful, offline app.

Built for every Luo speaker at home and abroad:

• Full Bible — 66 books, 1,189 chapters, searchable in seconds.
• Works 100% offline — no internet needed after install.
• Words of Jesus in red — every word Christ spoke is highlighted.
• Reading themes — dark, sepia and light, with adjustable text size.
• Daily verse — a fresh, inspiring "Wes Ma Kawuono" every single day.
• Jiwo (encouragement) — tell the app how you feel ("Arwaki! Iwinjo nade
  kawuono?") and it replies with a verse that speaks to your heart, in Luo,
  English, Kiswahili or Sheng.
• Share verses — create a beautiful verse card and share to WhatsApp,
  Facebook, Instagram or X, or save it to your phone.
• Book introductions for every book — author, place, date and outline.
• Bookmarks — keep your favourite verses.

Muma Maler is free, with no ads and no account required. Your bookmarks stay
on your phone — nothing is collected or shared.

Rwak e piny Luo duto — Gen 3:16, Zaburi 23, Isaya 40:31, kod weche mang'eny.
"""

*(The final line is a small Luo touch: "Carried across the whole Luo world —
John 3:16, Psalm 23, Isaiah 40:31, and many more.")*
